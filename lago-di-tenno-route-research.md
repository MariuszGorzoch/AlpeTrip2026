## Route Overview

Lago di Tenno is a spectacular turquoise alpine lake located in Trentino, Italy, approximately 15 km north-northwest of Arco. The lake sits at ~570m elevation in a narrow valley between limestone mountains. Nearby is the medieval village of Canale di Tenno, which is also accessible from Arco via gravel and paved roads.

### Key Geographic Points:
- **Starting Point**: Arco, Italy (45.90°N, 10.88°E) - 90m elevation
- **Destination**: Lago di Tenno (45.90°N, 10.83°E) - 570m elevation
- **Route Type**: Circular loop starting/ending in Arco
- **Nearest Village**: Canale di Tenno - medieval hilltop village
- **Best Season**: May-October (Alpine climate, snow possible Nov-April)

The main cycling corridors from Arco to Tenno follow these primary road systems:

**Route 3: Alternate High Road (Via Monte Cassian)**
- **Feature**: Higher elevation alternative
- **Advantage**: Panoramic views of Lake Garda
- **Terrain**: Mix of sealed roads and gravel tracks
- **Challenge**: Additional 200m+ elevation gain

## Road Names and Numbers (Access Infrastructure)

### Provincial Roads (Strada Provinciale - SP):

1. **SP4** - Main provincial road connecting Arco to Tenno area
   - Connects Arco with Tenno municipality
   - Partially sealed, with gravel alternative sections
   - Steep sections (6-8% gradient)
   - Primary vehicle route

2. **SP89** - Secondary provincial road
   - Alternative routing through Garda region
   - Less used by cars
   - Better for gravel cycling

3. **Local Mountain Roads**:
   - Via Canale di Tenno (through village)
   - Multiple unnamed strade bianche (white roads/gravel tracks)
   - Historic mule tracks (mulattiera)

### Road Surface Types:
- **Asphalt/Paved**: SP4 main sections, village roads
- **Gravel (Strada Bianca)**: 40-50% of typical route
- **Dirt/Earth Tracks**: 10-15% (seasonal, may be muddy)
- **Mixed Stone/Compacted**: 20-30%

## Practical Cycling Notes

### Difficulty Factors:
1. **Alpine Terrain**: Sustained climbing, significant elevation gain
2. **Mixed Surfaces**: Requires gravel-capable bike (gravel or MTB)
3. **Exposure**: Narrow sections with steep drop-offs near Canale
4. **Isolation**: Limited services between Arco and Lago (1-2 bars/restaurants)
5. **Weather**: Rapid weather changes at altitude, afternoon thunderstorms common

### Best Bike Setup:
- **Gravel Bike**: 40-50mm tires, drop bars optional
- **MTB**: Hardtail preferred over full-suspension for efficiency
- **Tire Pressure**: 35-45 PSI (lower on rocky sections)

### Essential Supplies:
- Water capacity: 1.5-2L minimum
- Puncture kit: Gravel = higher puncture risk
- Navigation: Offline GPX on phone/GPS device (limited cell coverage)
- Repair tools: Multi-tool, pump, spare tube

### Timing Considerations:
- **Total Time**: 3.5-5.5 hours riding (depending on fitness/terrain skill)
- **Daylight**: Start by 8am for summer; earlier for spring/fall
- **Rest Stops**: Plan 2-3 breaks (no services after Arco for 10km)
